Captured ONNX detections around the two vehicle crossings in Intel IoT DevKit's person-bicycle-car-detection.mp4. Frames are zero-based; source video is 768x432 at 12 FPS. Detections were recorded at a 0.10 floor and replayed at the selected test threshold. The large gap between the two ranges is replayed with empty updates so old tracks expire.

Video source and license: https://github.com/intel-iot-devkit/sample-videos/blob/master/person-bicycle-car-detection.mp4 (Intel IoT DevKit, CC BY 4.0). The detection data was generated with the bundled YOLO11n model.
